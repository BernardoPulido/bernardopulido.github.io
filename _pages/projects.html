Government of Ireland Postdoctoral Fellowship

Principal Investigator · Grant GOIPD/2026/3309

Efficient Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning with Homomorphic Encryption in Cloud Computing

Research Ireland · National College of Ireland · 2026–2028

Developing efficient privacy-preserving machine learning models that operate directly on encrypted data using homomorphic encryption. The project combines cryptographically computable non-linear functions with high-performance computing and hardware acceleration, with applications in medical imaging and smart transportation.
